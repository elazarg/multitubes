# Conic finance with transaction costs

This domain places a portfolio space at each time, scenario, or market regime and uses solvency
cones to compare portfolios. Trading and liquidation give linear, set-valued, or relational
transfers. Exact, lax, and oplax constraints suggest replication, superhedging, and subhedging;
closed walks suggest round-trip arbitrage tests.

Cone separation, probability, adaptedness, stochastic integration, measurable selection, and the
financial no-arbitrage interpretation remain domain-specific.
