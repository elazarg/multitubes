# Domain dictionary

This dictionary records what the common transport vocabulary means in each application. Its
status labels distinguish checked interfaces from terminology and future directions:

- **Proved** means Lean contains the stated adapter, equivalence, or consequence.
- **Interpretation** means the name faithfully describes the formal definition, but no theorem
  identifies it with a larger external theory.
- **Prospective** means the dossier has no compiled Lean client yet.

Start with [core concepts in domain language](THEORY_DICTIONARY.md) for the generic definitions,
conditions, and limits. The tables below link to the application sources containing the named
declarations. A **Proved** entry certifies those particular statements, not an entire field.

## Compiled applications

| Domain | Carrier and arrows | Sections, closure, and certificates | Status and limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Switched and hybrid control](SwitchedHybridControl/SwitchedHybridControl/Control.lean) | A vertex is a mode. The concrete transport fiber is its physical state space; the storage transport fiber is a space of functions on those states. A physical edge maps source states to target states; a walk is a switched or hybrid execution. `storageTransport` reverses that physical edge and pulls a target storage function back to its source. | `IsStorageCertificate` is exactly a lax section of the reversed storage transport (`storageCertificate_iff_isLaxSection`). `IsLyapunovFamily` is its zero-decrease case. `ErrorComparison` and `IsRadiusFamily` give local affine error bounds and `walk_error_le` propagates them. `leastErrorRadius` is the least Bellman radius above lower data. `IsContractiveGauge` scales modes to expose contraction. Rational Farkas weights certify infeasibility of bounded radius constraints. | **Proved.** “Lyapunov” here means transition nonincrease; positivity, coercivity, and stability are not asserted. A Bellman fixed point takes the maximum of incoming demands and does not make every edge tight. An infeasibility certificate rejects the comparison constraints, not the physical system. See [ErrorBounds.lean](SwitchedHybridControl/SwitchedHybridControl/ErrorBounds.lean), [LeastRadius.lean](SwitchedHybridControl/SwitchedHybridControl/LeastRadius.lean), and [RationalRadius.lean](SwitchedHybridControl/SwitchedHybridControl/RationalRadius.lean). |
| [Riccati filtering](RiccatiFiltering/RiccatiFiltering/CovariancePrediction.lean) | Fibers are positive-semidefinite covariance matrices ordered by the Löwner order, or scalar nonnegative priors. Edges are covariance prediction or scalar Riccati updates; paths are update sequences. | An exact section satisfies the update equation edgewise. A lax or exact edge gives an upper covariance bound along paths (`prediction_walkMap_le_of_lax_or_exact`); oplax or exact gives a lower bound (`prediction_le_walkMap_of_oplax_or_exact`). Scalar analogues are in [ScalarRiccati.lean](RiccatiFiltering/RiccatiFiltering/ScalarRiccati.lean). | **Proved.** These are covariance-order bounds. The application does not construct a stochastic filter, likelihood model, or Riccati fixed-point solver. |
| [Program semantics](ProgramSemantics/ProgramSemantics/PredicateTransport.lean) | Fibers are predicates, represented as sets of states. A forward instruction acts by direct image; a backward predicate transformer acts by inverse image on the reversed control-flow graph. A path is an execution trace. | Exact sections are predicates reproduced exactly by each transformer. Forward lax sections are inductive postconditions; oplax reverses containment. `mixedPredicateLaxification_iff` formally relates backward mixed constraints to forward lax constraints. `leastReachablePredicate` is the least inductive closure. `reachableStates_eq_leastLaxMajorant`, `computedReachability_sound`, and `walkMap_le_computedReachability` connect executable finite abstract saturation to concrete reachability through an `UpperSimulation`. | **Proved.** See [FiniteReachability.lean](ProgramSemantics/ProgramSemantics/FiniteReachability.lean) and [AbstractAnalysis.lean](ProgramSemantics/ProgramSemantics/AbstractAnalysis.lean). The parity analysis is sound and intentionally imprecise; it is not a completeness theorem for arbitrary abstractions. |
| [Mathematical morphology](MathematicalMorphology/MathematicalMorphology/RelationalTransport.lean) | Fibers are sets of signals or features. A relation acts forward by existential image, called dilation, and backward by universal core, called erosion. Erosion therefore lives on reversed edges. | `mixedErosionLaxification_iff` turns mixed erosion constraints into forward lax dilation constraints. `leastDilationEnvelope` is the least dilation-invariant family above lower data. | **Proved.** The envelope closes seeds under the chosen relations. Translation invariance or a geometric structuring element is not assumed. |
| [Monotone co-design](MonotoneCoDesign/MonotoneCoDesign/MonotoneFeasibility.lean) | Fibers are ordered spaces of interface values. Each edge is a feasibility relation, downward closed in required functionality and upward closed in permitted resources; paths compose these relations. | `IsMonotoneFeasibility` says requiring less functionality and permitting more resources preserve feasibility. `HasMonotoneFeasibility.walkRelation_concat` and `walkRelation_of_pos` propagate this order behavior through relational paths. | **Proved** for the relational and monotonicity statements. “Co-design” is an interpretation; the client does not implement optimization, Pareto-front algorithms, or numerical design search. |
| [Cellular sheaves and sensors](CellularSheaves/CellularSheaves/GraphSheaf.lean) | Vertex and edge stalks are the fibers. Incidence arrows are restriction maps; incidence paths compose restrictions. `relationTransport` retains a possibly nonfunctional compatibility span. | `compatibleSectionEquiv` proves that compatible vertex assignments are exact sections of the bipartite incidence transport, and `isRelationSection_iff` gives the relational formulation. For affine sensor readings, zero tolerance is exact compatibility; nonzero tolerance is encoded by signed rational row inequalities and admits rational Farkas certificates. [OptimalTolerance.lean](CellularSheaves/CellularSheaves/OptimalTolerance.lean) characterizes and attains the minimum tolerance. [CycleConsistency.lean](CellularSheaves/CellularSheaves/CycleConsistency.lean) gives the unit-scale signed-cycle criterion. [ActiveCertificate.lean](CellularSheaves/CellularSheaves/ActiveCertificate.lean) uses `exists_rankSparse_activeCertificate` to select a normalized balanced certificate on worst signed comparisons, with support at most the signed-row rank plus one. | **Proved.** Minimizing the maximum absolute affine residual is **Chebyshev, or infinity-norm minimax, affine fitting**. This is an **Interpretation** matching the defined objective; see [Boyd and Vandenberghe’s convex optimization slides](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/bv_cvxslides.pdf). The active support is an optimality explanation, not ordered polynomial alternation, a minimum-cardinality explanation, or a diagnosis of sensor malfunction. |
| [Stochastic processes](StochasticProcesses/StochasticProcesses/KernelTransport.lean) | Fibers are extended-nonnegative observables on state spaces. A forward probability kernel induces expectation pullback on the reversed graph; reversed paths compose those pullbacks. | `isSection_iff_harmonic` identifies exact sections with harmonic observable families, and `isLaxSection_iff_superharmonic` identifies lax sections with superharmonic families. In [HittingBarrier.lean](StochasticProcesses/StochasticProcesses/HittingBarrier.lean), a superharmonic barrier bounds every finite-horizon hitting value and their supremum `eventuallyHit`. | **Proved.** `eventuallyHit` is an order-theoretic supremum of finite horizons. No infinite-path probability measure is built, so harmonicity is not identified with a martingale theorem. |
| [Quantum channels](QuantumChannels/QuantumTransport/Kraus.lean) | Fibers are positive semidefinite matrices ordered by the Löwner order. Edges apply a Kraus family; paths compose those positive maps. | `krausMap_posSemidef` and `monotone_krausMap` establish positivity and order preservation. `walkMap_le_of_lax_or_exact` and `le_walkMap_of_oplax_or_exact` propagate upper and lower matrix bounds. | **Proved** for finite-dimensional matrices and the stated Kraus formulas. [Discard.lean](QuantumChannels/QuantumTransport/Discard.lean) proves `not_exists_galoisConnection_discard`, its right-handed analogue, and `not_exists_discardResidual`: a linear-algebraic adjoint formula must not be confused with an order residual. An arbitrary Kraus family is not assumed trace preserving; no general normalized-channel or infinite-dimensional theory is asserted. |
| [Conic finance](ConicFinance/ConicFinance/TransactionCosts.lean) | The carrier is a portfolio additive group. Each edge has an admissible increment set; a path accumulates increments by Minkowski-style addition. | `walkRelation_iff_sub_mem_walkIncrement` characterizes route feasibility by the accumulated increment, and `walkIncrement_append` proves composition. `convex_walkIncrement` preserves convexity when each edge increment set is convex. The alternative-route example proves that convexifying a union can introduce an unattainable portfolio (`zero_mem_convexHull_alternativeReachable`, `zero_not_mem_alternativeReachable`). | **Proved** for these transaction relations and convexity statements. Replication, superhedging, no-arbitrage, pricing measures, and the fundamental theorem of asset pricing are not formalized. |
| [Coalgebraic refinement](CoalgebraicRefinement/CoalgebraicRefinement/Deterministic.lean) | The transport fiber is the lattice of relations between two deterministic labelled systems. Its single loop applies `simulationOperator`; walks count unfoldings. Inside that operator, each label advances both systems; `DeterministicSystem.run` evaluates finite words. | `IsSimulation` is a post-fixed point of `simulationOperator`; `greatestSimulation` is its greatest fixed point. `greatestSimulation_iff_traceRelated` identifies it with the specified output relation holding after every finite word; equality is one possible choice. `isOplaxSection_iff_isSimulation` is the formal transport adapter, and `simulation_le_walkMap` propagates simulation through a finite path. | **Proved** for deterministic finite-word trace semantics. It does not cover arbitrary coalgebraic functors or infinite traces. |
| [Games and model checking](GamesModelChecking/GamesModelChecking/Alternation.lean) | The formal carrier is a pair of complete lattices in `BinaryMonotoneSystem`, with two mutually dependent monotone maps. It is not a formal space of plays, priorities, players, or winning regions. | `leastGreatestSolution` and `greatestLeastSolution` implement different nested least/greatest fixed-point orders. For the copy system, `copyFixedPoint_iff_isSection` relates local transport sections to simultaneous fixed points. `priority_data_not_determined_by_local_sections` proves that local section data cannot recover the fixed-point priority order. | **Proved** for monotone fixed-point alternation and the non-encoding result. The game/model-checking terminology is an **Interpretation**; there is no parity-game or progress-measure adapter. Priority is not lax/oplax polarity. |
| [Obstacle problems](ObstacleProblems/ObstacleProblems.lean) | The carrier is a real continuation value. The clamped affine operator has lower and upper obstacles around an affine continuation branch; iteration is the finite-horizon recurrence. | `IsObstacleSolution` is the clamped fixed-point equation. `existsUnique_obstacleSolution` and `tendsto_iterate_obstacleSolution` give uniqueness and convergence under a contractive slope. `obstacleSolution_contact_or_continuation` proves lower contact, upper contact, or strict continuation. | **Proved.** “Stopping game” is motivation only: no stopping time, strategy space, payoff process, or game-value equivalence is formalized. |
| [Discrete-event systems](DiscreteEventSystems/DiscreteEventSystems/Synchronization.lean) | Fibers are real event times. An edge is a precedence dependency carrying a delay; a path is a dependency chain. The vertex update takes the maximum incoming demand. | `TimedNetwork.IsSchedule` is the max-plus eigen-equation. `iterate_eigenschedule` gives linear firing-time growth. In [CertifiedSchedule.lean](DiscreteEventSystems/DiscreteEventSystems/CertifiedSchedule.lean), a maximizing critical cycle constructs an optimal rooted phase; cycle mean is a lower-bound certificate for every feasible cycle time. | **Proved.** An eigen-schedule makes the maximum incoming branch exact, not every dependency tight. The construction is mathematical rather than an executable cycle-search algorithm, and throughput uses floorless unit-slope labels. |
| [Quantitative relations](QuantitativeRelations/QuantitativeRelations/GradedTransport.lean) | Fibers carry a family of relations indexed by a grade. Each edge has a local grade; a path uses the **sum** of its edge grades. | `IsGradedMixedSectionFor.walkMap_rel_of_lax_or_exact`, its oplax analogue, and the symmetric result compose local relatedness along walks. In the metric instance, `walkMap_nndist_le` and `closedWalk_nndist_le` bound nonnegative distance by the summed grade. | **Proved** for additive grades and the nonnegative-distance instance. The package supplies no privacy interpretation, neighboring-dataset relation, randomized mechanism, divergence, or differential-privacy theorem. It also states no generic “grade zero means exact section” adapter. |
| [Web of trust](WebOfTrust/WebOfTrust/ProvenanceTransport.lean) | Fibers are sets of evidence tokens. Provenance edges use union-preserving evidence transfers. [ThresholdPolicy.lean](WebOfTrust/WebOfTrust/ThresholdPolicy.lean) instead uses a monotone conjunctive rule that needs two tokens together and therefore does not preserve unions. | `IsSoundAssignment` is the lax closure condition. `mem_soundClosure_iff` and `soundClosure_isLeast` characterize provenance closure. `thresholdSweep_two_isLeast` proves that two finite covering sweeps reach a sound fixed assignment containing the seeds and below every such assignment. The core separately handles fair schedules under product ACC. | **Proved** for provenance, the finite threshold computation, and the core worklist theorems. “Web of trust” is an **Interpretation**. Evidence tokens do not formalize identity assurance, cryptographic validation, adversarial resistance, or probabilistic confidence. |

## Reading the common words

The same formal word can acquire different domain names only after its carrier, order, and edge
orientation are fixed. In particular, “exact” always means equality for the chosen transport;
“lax” and “oplax” refer to its order direction; and least invariant closure refers to the encoded edge constraints and prescribed seeds.
The [core dictionary](THEORY_DICTIONARY.md) separates path joins, least fixed points,
and Bellman equality, with the hypotheses needed to relate them.
