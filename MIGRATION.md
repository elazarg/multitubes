# Legacy name and imports

Multitube was previously published as `directed-transport`, with its main modules under
`Maths.DirectedTransport`. The repository is now `multitube`, and those modules are under
`Maths.Multitube`; declaration namespaces such as `Maths.Transport` remain unchanged.

UniformEquilibrium, the original downstream client, may continue to refer to the former
repository and module paths until its integration is updated. This note is the temporary record
of that correspondence.
